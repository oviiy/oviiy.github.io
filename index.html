About me

I’m Michael (Xihao), a quantitative analyst working on fixed income, credit derivatives, and model validation in production systems. I care about turning market conventions and research ideas into pricing and risk analytics that teams can rely on.

I currently work at Clearwater Analytics on fixed income quantitative research and validation. Before that I was at MSCI in Berkeley, where I built and maintained credit pricing models — CDS, credit indices, options, and tranches — including a bespoke / SRT tranche model from prototype to production.

I hold an M.S. in Mathematical Finance from USC and a B.E. in Electrical Engineering from UNSW (with an exchange semester at NYU).

Outside work I fish when I can get to the water, play tennis, and spend a lot of time in Dota 2 (still chasing better MMR). I also build small browser games for fun — a sample is Three Body, an orbital sandbox inspired by the novel.
