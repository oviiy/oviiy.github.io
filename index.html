About me

I’m Michael (Xihao), a quantitative analyst working on fixed income, credit derivatives, multi-asset allocation, and model validation in production systems. I care about turning market conventions and research ideas into pricing and risk analytics that teams can rely on.

I currently work at Clearwater Analytics on fixed income quantitative research, model development, and validation. Before that I spent 3.5 years at MSCI in Berkeley building and maintaining credit pricing models — CDS, credit indices, options, and tranches — including a bespoke / SRT tranche model from prototype to production.

I hold an M.S. in Mathematical Finance from USC and a B.E. in Electrical Engineering from UNSW (with an exchange semester at NYU).

Outside work I fish when I can get to the water, hike, play tennis, and have fun in Dota 2. I like exploring good local bakeries and food wherever I am. I also enjoy building games — a sample is Particell, a small particle playground in the browser. At home I share the place with two cats: CoCo (Ragdoll, 3 yrs) and Gaga (domestic longhair, 7 yrs) — more photos in the Gallery.
